On election day, the most important people in the party are not on a stage. They are at the polling stations, from the moment voting opens until the results are declared.

## What a polling agent does

A polling agent represents the party at a polling station. Agents:

- watch the setting up of the polling station and the opening of voting,
- observe the voting process throughout the day,
- witness the counting of ballots, and
- check and sign the declared results for their station.

It is a serious responsibility, and it needs people who are calm, careful and willing to stay the whole day.

## Training comes first

No one should arrive at a polling station unprepared. Volunteers will be trained on the electoral rules, what to watch for, how to raise a concern properly, and how to record and report results. Training dates will be posted on our [events page](/events).

## Who can volunteer

We need agents across all 15 electoral areas, from Anloga to Anwomaso. If you are reliable and you care about a free and fair process, we want to hear from you. Students at KNUST are especially welcome.
