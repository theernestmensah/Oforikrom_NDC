Akwaaba. This is the new home of the National Democratic Congress in Oforikrom Constituency on the web.

Oforikrom is a big, busy and young constituency. It runs from Anloga and Oforikrom town, through Bomso, Ayigya and the KNUST campus, out to Kentinkrono, Ayeduase and Anwomaso. Our members live and work in all 15 electoral areas, and until now there was no single place to find out who to talk to, what is happening, or how to help.

## What you can do here

- **Find your branch.** Type your community into the [branch finder](/#branches) to see your electoral area and zonal council, and how to reach the people who organise it.
- **Join the party.** The [join form](/get-involved#join) takes two minutes. Your details go straight to the constituency secretariat, who will connect you with your branch.
- **Volunteer.** From polling station agents to communications, we need people with every kind of skill. See the [volunteer roles](/get-involved#volunteer).
- **Follow the news and events.** We will post constituency news, explainers on government programmes, and dates for meetings and training.

## Built for phones

Most people in Oforikrom will read this site on a phone, often on mobile data. We have kept it light and fast, and you can share any page straight to WhatsApp.

## Tell us what you think

This site belongs to the members. If something is missing, wrong or could be better, [contact us](/contact) and let us know.
