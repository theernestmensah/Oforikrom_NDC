The party is only as strong as its branches. Before you can help win a polling station, you need to know which one is yours. This guide explains how Oforikrom is organised.

## Four zonal councils

The Oforikrom Municipal Assembly, created in 2018, groups the constituency's electoral areas into four zonal councils:

- **Oforikrom:** Anloga East, Anloga West, Oforikrom East and Oforikrom West.
- **Bomso:** Bomso, and Tech (the KNUST campus).
- **Ayigya:** Ayigya Zongo, Ahenbronum and Ayigya Akatego.
- **Kentinkrono / Ayeduase:** Kentinkrono / Oduom / Nsenie, Aprade / Meseum, Emena / Boadi / Appiadu / Kokoben, Anwomaso / Bebre, Deduako / Kodiekrom / Apemso, and Ayeduase / Kotei / Twumduase.

The Kentinkrono / Ayeduase zone is by far the largest, and it is growing fast as new homes and hostels go up on the eastern side of Kumasi.

## Why your branch matters

Every polling station has a branch of the party, with its own executives. Branches recruit members, organise canvassing, and put trained agents at the polling station on election day. When we talk about winning Oforikrom, we mean winning it one polling station at a time.

## How to find yours

1. Open the [branch finder](/#branches) and type your community, for example Kotei, Bomso or KNUST.
2. Note your electoral area and zonal council.
3. Use the contact link on the card, or [message the constituency office](/contact), and we will put you in touch with your branch executives.

If you have recently moved, or you are a student registered elsewhere, tell us. We can still find a way for you to help here in Oforikrom.

*Electoral area names follow the Oforikrom Municipal Assembly's published list. The Electoral Commission may revise boundaries in future, and we will update this guide if it does.*
